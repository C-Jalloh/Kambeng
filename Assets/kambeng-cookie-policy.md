**Last updated: June 19, 2026**

## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site function properly, remember your preferences, and understand how the site is used.

## 2. How We Use Cookies

Kambeng (kambeng.hexai.gm) uses cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage) for the following purposes:

### 2.1 Essential Cookies
Required for the Platform to function — e.g. keeping you logged in, maintaining your session during a donation or signup flow, and enabling core security features. The Platform will not work properly without these.

### 2.2 Functional Cookies
Remember your preferences, such as language or display settings, to improve your experience.

### 2.3 Analytics Cookies
Help us understand how visitors use the Platform (e.g. pages visited, time on site) so we can improve performance and usability. These may be set by us or by third-party analytics providers.

### 2.4 Payment & Fraud-Prevention Cookies
Set by our payment partners — **Wave**, **APS/Afrimoney**, and **Stripe** — when you make a donation or process a card payment. These cookies help process transactions securely and detect fraudulent activity. They are governed by each provider's own privacy and cookie policies.

## 3. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are placed by third parties who provide services on our behalf, including:
- **Stripe** — for secure card payment processing.
- **Wave** and **APS/Afrimoney** — for mobile money transaction processing.
- Analytics providers — to help us understand site usage.

We do not control these third-party cookies directly; please refer to each provider's policy for details.

## 4. Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you:
- View what cookies are stored and delete them individually.
- Block third-party cookies.
- Block all cookies from specific sites or all sites.

Note that blocking essential cookies may prevent parts of the Platform — such as login or donation processing — from working correctly.
