# Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Kambeng — operated by HexAI**
**Last updated: June 19, 2026**

## 1. General Principle

Donations made through Kambeng are voluntary contributions to a campaigner's cause and are, as a general rule, **non-refundable**. Because funds may be transferred directly to a campaigner's mobile money wallet or withdrawn shortly after donation, Kambeng's ability to reverse a transaction is limited once funds have moved.

## 2. When a Refund May Be Possible

We will review refund requests in the following situations:

- **Duplicate or accidental charge:** if you were charged more than once for the same donation due to a technical error.
- **Unauthorized transaction:** if you did not authorize the payment (e.g. your card or mobile money account was used without your permission).
- **Failed or incomplete transaction:** if funds were debited from your account but the donation did not register on the Platform.
- **Fraudulent campaign:** if a campaign is found, after investigation, to be fraudulent or to have misrepresented its purpose, Kambeng may — at its discretion — work with the relevant payment processor to attempt recovery, where technically and legally possible.

Refund eligibility is assessed case by case and is not guaranteed, particularly where funds have already been withdrawn by a campaigner via mobile money.

## 3. How to Request a Refund

Contact support@hexai.gm within **14 days** of the transaction, including:
- Your account email or phone number.
- The campaign name and donation date/amount.
- A description of the issue.
- A screenshot or transaction reference, where available.

We will investigate and respond within a reasonable time, generally within **10 business days**.

## 4. Card Payments (Stripe)

For donations made by card, refunds — where approved — are processed back to the original card via Stripe and may take **5–10 business days** to reflect on your statement, depending on your bank.

## 5. Mobile Money Payments (Wave / APS-Afrimoney)

For donations made via Wave or APS mobile money, refunds depend on whether the funds remain in the campaigner's wallet or with the payment processor. If funds have already been withdrawn by the campaigner, Kambeng cannot guarantee recovery, but we will assist in good faith and may pursue recovery directly with the campaigner where appropriate.

## 6. Chargebacks

If you initiate a chargeback with your card issuer instead of contacting us first, your Kambeng account may be suspended pending resolution. We encourage donors to contact us directly first, as chargebacks can take longer to resolve and may affect a campaigner's ability to access legitimately raised funds while under dispute.

## 7. Campaigner Payout Reversals

If a campaign is found to violate our Terms & Conditions (e.g. fraud, misrepresentation, prohibited content), Kambeng reserves the right to:
- Suspend further payouts to the campaigner.
- Withhold or reverse pending payouts.
- Cooperate with payment processors and, where necessary, law enforcement to recover misappropriated funds.

## 8. Platform Fees

Platform fees and payment processor fees (Wave, APS, Stripe) are generally non-refundable, even where a donation refund is approved, unless required otherwise by law.
