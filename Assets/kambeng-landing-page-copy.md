## Hero Section

**Headline:**
# Real Causes. Real Proof. Real Gambians.

**Subheadline:**
Donate to verified campaigns in seconds — with Wave, APS Mobile Money, or your card. Every cedi tracked, every campaigner ID-verified, every naira accounted for with receipts.

**Primary CTA:** Donate to a Campaign →
**Secondary CTA:** Start Your Own Campaign

**Microcopy under CTAs (reduces friction):**
> No account needed to donate · Takes under 2 minutes

**Why this works:** Your original headline ("Fund What Matters in The Gambia") is good but generic — it describes the platform, not the donor's experience. This version leads with the trust mechanism (proof, verification) because that's the #1 objection donors have with crowdfunding platforms: "how do I know this is real?" It also puts donating first in the CTA order, since donors are a larger and lower-friction audience than campaign creators — converting them first builds the social proof that gets campaigners to trust you next.

## "Built on Trust" Section

**Section headline:**
## You'll Never Wonder Where Your Money Went

**Section subhead:**
Every campaign comes with verified identity, photo proof, and spending receipts — because asking "did this actually help?" shouldn't be necessary.

### Card 1 — KYC Verified Identity
**Label:** 100% ID-Verified
**Copy:** Every campaigner submits a government ID and is manually verified before they can raise a single cedi. No anonymous accounts, no fake campaigns.

### Card 2 — Photo & Receipt Proof
**Label:** Public Proof
**Copy:** Campaigners upload photos and receipts showing exactly how funds were spent — visible to every donor, forever. If a campaigner can't show proof, you'll see it.

### Card 3 — Pay However You Want
**Label:** 3 Ways to Pay
**Copy:** Donate instantly with Wave, APS Mobile Money, or a Visa/Mastercard via secure card checkout. No app required, no waiting — funds reach campaigners directly.

### Card 4 — Public Donor Reviews
**Label:** Always Visible
**Copy:** Donors rate and review every campaign publicly. A campaigner's track record is permanent — good or bad.

**Why this section works:** Your original copy described features ("Wave Mobile Money — payments go directly to the campaigner's wallet"). This version reframes each feature as an answer to a donor's unspoken doubt. "0% hidden fees" became "Pay however you want" because once you add APS and Stripe, "0% hidden fees" stops being accurate for card payments (Stripe takes a cut) — don't promise something you can't keep across all three rails.

**Why this works:** Your original final CTA was symmetrical ("Start a Campaign" first, "Browse Campaigns" second) — same priority issue as the hero. ## Quick Conversion Checklist (beyond copy)

1. **Lead with donating, not campaign creation** — donors convert faster and at higher volume than campaigners; campaigners follow once they see activity.
2. **Show the payment logos early** — Wave, APS, Visa/Mastercard badges near the hero CTA reduce "will my payment method even work here?" drop-off.
3. **Populate the stats bar** — even modest real numbers beat placeholders; an empty "—" reads as "nobody uses this yet."
4. **Add the new Payment Methods section** — once you support 3 rails, donors need to see all 3 before they commit, especially card users who may assume it's mobile-money-only (your current copy says so explicitly).
5. **Link legal pages in the footer** — required before processing card payments via Stripe, and builds trust for KYC-sensitive users.
