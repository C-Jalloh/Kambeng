# Privacy Policy

**Kambeng — operated by HexAI**
**Last updated: June 19, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Kambeng ("Kambeng," "we," "us," or "our") is a crowdfunding platform operated by HexAI, based in The Gambia. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your personal information when you use kambeng.hexai.gm and related services (the "Platform").

By using the Platform, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the Platform.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide
- **Account information:** name, email address, phone number, password, and profile details.
- **KYC (Know Your Customer) information:** government-issued ID, date of birth, and other identity-verification documents required for campaigners before they can receive or withdraw funds.
- **Campaign information:** campaign descriptions, photos, receipts, proof of expenditure, and updates you post.
- **Payment information:** mobile money account details (e.g. Wave, APS/Afrimoney), and, where applicable, card details processed through our payment partner Stripe. We do not store full card numbers — these are handled directly by Stripe.
- **Communications:** messages you send us, support requests, and reviews or feedback left on campaigns.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- Device information, IP address, browser type, and operating system.
- Usage data such as pages visited, time spent, and actions taken on the Platform.
- Cookies and similar technologies (see our [Cookie Policy] for details).

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties
- Verification results from KYC/identity-verification providers.
- Transaction confirmations from payment processors (Wave, APS/Afrimoney, Stripe).

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Create and manage your account.
- Verify campaigner identity (KYC) before enabling fundraising or withdrawals.
- Process donations and payouts via Wave, APS mobile money, and Stripe.
- Display campaign proof (photos, receipts) and donor reviews publicly, where you've chosen to share them.
- Detect and prevent fraud, money laundering, and abuse of the Platform.
- Communicate with you about your account, campaigns, transactions, and platform updates.
- Improve and secure the Platform.
- Comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

## 4. How We Share Your Information

We share information with:
- **Payment processors** (Wave, APS/Afrimoney, Stripe) — to process donations, payouts, and card transactions. Each processor has its own privacy policy governing how it handles payment data.
- **Identity verification providers** — to confirm KYC submissions.
- **Service providers** who help us operate the Platform (e.g. hosting, analytics, customer support tools), under confidentiality obligations.
- **Other users** — campaign descriptions, photos, receipts, proof of expenditure, and your public donor reviews are visible to anyone using the Platform. Your KYC documents are never made public.
- **Law enforcement or regulators**, where required by law, to prevent fraud, or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

We do not sell your personal information.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as your account is active, and as needed to comply with legal, accounting, or KYC/AML obligations — typically for a minimum period after account closure as required by applicable financial regulations. Campaign records (photos, receipts, proof of expenditure) may remain publicly visible after a campaign ends, for transparency and audit purposes.

## 6. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:
- Access the personal information we hold about you.
- Request correction of inaccurate information.
- Request deletion of your information, subject to our legal and regulatory retention obligations (e.g. KYC records cannot be deleted while regulations require their retention).
- Withdraw consent for optional processing (e.g. marketing communications).

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email listed in Section 10.

## 7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information, including encryption in transit, restricted access to KYC documents, and secure handling of payment data through PCI-compliant processors (Stripe) and licensed mobile money partners (Wave, APS/Afrimoney). No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. International Transfers

Some of our service providers (including payment processors) may process data outside The Gambia. Where this occurs, we take steps to ensure your information continues to receive an appropriate level of protection.

## 9. Children's Privacy

The Platform is not directed at individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us information, contact us so we can remove it.
